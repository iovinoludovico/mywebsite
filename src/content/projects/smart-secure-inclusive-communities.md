Funding obtained by the GSSI Computer Science area under PON AIM1880573 – 2, *Action I.2 "AIM – Attraction and International Mobility"*.

- **Line 1 – Researcher mobility.** An RTD-a researcher position in cultural heritage, using model-driven techniques to design a digital ecosystem that supports the digital transition of cultural heritage and its transmission in space and time. It lets citizens interact more closely and in different ways with heritage, creating more inclusive cultural environments, especially for people with difficulties in perception, memory, cognition and communication. The position was awarded to Dr. Martina De Sanctis.
- **Line 2 – Researcher attraction.** An RTD-a researcher position on smart, secure and inclusive communities and technology transfer, with focus on data analytics, big data, machine learning and data science. The goal is advanced technological solutions that modernize the services offered to citizens, with smart infrastructures that make communication between institutions and citizens effective and targeted, and that support data-driven decision making. The position was awarded to Dr. Giorgio Manganini.
